IPTV+

A simple, fast media player for TVs, Android TV boxes and Mac.

  • Plays your own M3U and Xtream playlists. IPTV+ does not provide any channels or content.
  • TV guide, catch-up, movies and series with posters, trailers and subtitles.
  • Optional Google sign-in keeps favorites and watch progress the same on all your devices, stored in your own Google Drive.
